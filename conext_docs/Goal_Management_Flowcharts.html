01

Big picture

One quarter, start → finish.

flowchart LR
  A([Quarter starts]) --> B[People write goals]
  B --> C[Manager reviews]
  C --> D[Day 30: hard lock]
  D --> E[Update progress]
  E --> F[Manager gives score]
  F --> J([Person sees score])
02

Who sets goals this quarter

Joined on/before Day 1. Probation does not matter.

flowchart LR
  A([New person]) --> B{Joined on Day 1 or before?}
  B -->|Yes| C[Must set goals]
  B -->|No| D[Not eligible this quarter]
  D --> E([Starts next quarter])
03

Write goals

Employee · first 30 days.

flowchart LR
  A([Open goals]) --> C[Write goals]
  C --> D{2+ goals?}
  D -->|No| E[Cannot submit]
  E --> C
  D -->|Yes| F{Weights = 100%?}
  F -->|No| E
  F -->|Yes| G{Has measures?}
  G -->|No| E
  G -->|Yes| H([Submit all])
  H --> I[Pending for manager approval]
04

Manager review

All of one person’s goals, together.

flowchart LR
  A([Open list]) --> B[Pick person]
  B -->|Big changes| D[Send all back]
  D --> F[Person fixes]
  F --> A
  B -->|Small tweaks| G[Manager edits]
  G --> H[Person gets a change note]
  H --> J[Approve all]
  B -->|Looks good| J
  J --> L([Done])
05

Day 30 hard lock

No new submits. Pending approvals can still finish.

flowchart LR
  A([Day 30]) -->|Not submitted| C[Incomplete · score = 0]
  A -->|Approved| D[Goals locked]
  A -->|Submitted · not approved| E[Stay pending]
  E --> F[Direct manager can still approve]
  E --> G[HRBP delegates to +1]
  G --> H[+1 can also approve]
  F --> I([Approved after lock])
  H --> I
06

Team or manager change

Person and goals move together.

flowchart LR
  A([Team or manager changes]) --> B[Employee moves]
  B --> C[Goals move with them]
  C --> D([New manager owns them])
07

Update progress

During the quarter, after lock.

flowchart LR
  A([Update progress]) --> B[Manager can look anytime]
  B --> C{Agrees?}
  C -->|Yes| D[Keep it]
  C -->|No| E[Manager corrects]
  A --> F{Blocked?}
  F -->|Yes| G[Tell manager in chat]
  F -->|No| I[Keep updating]
08

Quarter score

Q1 / Q2 / Q3. Q4 is in the yearly review.

flowchart LR
  A([Next quarter starts]) --> B[Manager opens check-in]
  B --> C[Shows % complete]
  C --> D[Manager gives score and submits]
  D --> G([Person sees score])
